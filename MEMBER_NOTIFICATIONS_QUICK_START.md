# Member Deal Response Notifications - Quick Start

## What You Get

When a trusted partner (business) approves or rejects your deal request, you'll receive an instant push notification on your phone with:

✅ **Business name** - Know exactly which business responded
✅ **Deal details** - See the deal name and amount
✅ **Next steps** - Clear instructions on how to proceed
✅ **Sound & vibration** - Won't miss important approvals

## Notification Examples

### ✅ Approved - Pay in App
```
Title: ✅ Deal Approved - Pay Now
Message: Woolworths approved your request for 20% Off Fresh Produce. 
         Tap to pay R80.00 now.
```
**Action**: Tap notification → Opens payment screen → Complete payment

---

### ✅ Approved - Pay in Store
```
Title: ✅ Deal Approved - Visit Store
Message: Pick n Pay approved your request for Buy 2 Get 1 Free (x3). 
         Visit the store to complete payment (R150.00).
```
**Action**: Visit the store → Show your QR code → Pay at counter → Get discount

---

### ❌ Request Declined
```
Title: ❌ Deal Request Declined
Message: Spar declined your request for Half Price Bread. 
         Reason: Product out of stock
```
**Action**: Try requesting a different deal or visit another time

## How It Works

```
1. You request a deal from a business
   ↓
2. Business reviews your request
   ↓
3. Business approves or rejects
   ↓
4. You get instant push notification
   ↓
5. You complete payment (approved) or try another deal (rejected)
```

## Notification Settings

### Android
1. **Ensure notifications are enabled**: Settings → Apps → Local Lekker → Notifications
2. **High priority**: Deal response notifications use high priority for timely delivery
3. **Sound/Vibration**: Customize in system notification settings
4. **Badge counter**: Shows unread notification count on app icon

### iOS
1. **Allow notifications**: Settings → Local Lekker → Notifications → Allow Notifications
2. **Banners**: Show as banners for immediate visibility
3. **Sounds**: Plays alert sound when notification arrives
4. **Badges**: App icon shows notification count

## Tips

💡 **Tap to act fast**: Approvals are time-sensitive - tap notification to pay immediately

💡 **Check reason for rejections**: Business may explain why (out of stock, expired deal, etc.)

💡 **Request again**: If rejected, you can submit a new request for a different deal

💡 **POS vs In-App**: Pay attention to payment method - some require store visit

💡 **Quantity matters**: Notifications show quantity (x3) when requesting multiple items

## Troubleshooting

### Not receiving notifications?

1. **Check notification permissions**
   - Android: Settings → Apps → Local Lekker → Notifications (ON)
   - iOS: Settings → Local Lekker → Allow Notifications (ON)

2. **Check internet connection**
   - Notifications require active connection
   - Works on WiFi or mobile data

3. **Check app is updated**
   - Ensure you have latest version from app store

4. **Restart app**
   - Close and reopen Local Lekker

### Notifications delayed?

1. **Battery optimization** (Android)
   - Settings → Apps → Local Lekker → Battery → Unrestricted

2. **Background app refresh** (iOS)
   - Settings → General → Background App Refresh → Local Lekker (ON)

3. **Do Not Disturb**
   - Check if DND mode is blocking notifications

## What Happens Next?

### After Approval (In-App Payment)
1. Tap notification
2. Review deal details
3. Complete payment in app
4. Receive digital receipt
5. Get instant discount applied

### After Approval (POS Payment)
1. Note the business and amount
2. Visit the store
3. Show your QR code to staff
4. Pay at counter
5. Staff scans receipt and applies discount

### After Rejection
1. Read rejection reason
2. Request different deal OR
3. Contact business for clarification
4. Try again later

## Privacy & Data

- Notifications include business name and deal details only
- Your payment info is never shown in notifications
- Tap notification to see full details securely in app
- Notifications auto-delete after 30 days
